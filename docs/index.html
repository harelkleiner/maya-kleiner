Hi! I'm Maya Kleiner

Designer & photographer. My work explores the intersection between typography, movement and meaning: clean, bold design that conveys ideas through precise form, contrast and color. My creative process focuses on conceptual thinking and the development of unique visual languages for each project, aiming to create identities that speak clearly, intelligently and emotionally.

Final project 2026

CORA

2026 DROP · Incubation brand

CORA explores the human need for moments of separation, stillness and processing in an overstimulated world. Inspired by the concept of incubation, a state in which the mind keeps processing and creating during rest, the brand creates a personal space between the individual and the outside environment.
