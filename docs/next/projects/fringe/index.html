06 / 10Branding & Typography

FRINGEFRINGE

This branding project was developed in collaboration with Expo Tel Aviv to mark its 100th anniversary, focusing on the artistic world of fringe theatre. The project aims to translate the spirit of fringe performance (experimental, free and at times unpredictable) into a contemporary visual language. Through expressive typography, dynamic compositions and strong contrasts, the branding reflects the tension between structure and rebellion, and between a long-standing cultural tradition and innovative stage creation.
