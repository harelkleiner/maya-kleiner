Hi! I'm Maya Kleiner

Designer & photographer. My work explores the intersection between typography, movement and meaning: clean, bold design that conveys ideas through precise form, contrast and color. My creative process focuses on conceptual thinking and the development of unique visual languages for each project, aiming to create identities that speak clearly, intelligently and emotionally.
