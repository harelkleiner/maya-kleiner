02Experimental Typography

SPIN 90

The project shows every letter of the Hebrew alphabet rotating around a fixed axis, at a uniform speed and the same 90-degree angle. The rotation effect was tested on the typefaces Haim, Frank-Ruhl and Miriam. Each letter changed form through the movement, losing its familiar shape and becoming an abstract, illegible image. The aim of the project was to explore the limits of legibility, and to examine how formal movement turns a letter from a familiar symbol into a visual object.
