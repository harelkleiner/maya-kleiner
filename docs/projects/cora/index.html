CORA

2026 DROP · Incubation brand

CORA explores the human need for moments of separation, stillness and processing in an overstimulated world. Inspired by the concept of incubation, a state in which the mind keeps processing and creating during rest, the brand creates a personal space between the individual and the outside environment. Through the ideas of protection, pause and reconnection, CORA transforms clothing into a personal cocoon: a soft boundary that lets the body disconnect from external noise and reconnect with the inner self. The brand views isolation not as an escape, but as a necessary condition for transformation, creativity and growth.

A garment as a personal space

In this project, I translated this idea into the world of the body and clothing, exploring how a garment can become a personal space: a protective layer that distances the wearer from external noise, overload and stimuli.

Concept

What is incubation?

Incubation describes a cognitive process in which neural networks continue to process and reorganize information during periods of rest, allowing new connections to form and enabling insights, ideas and creative thinking. The concept extends beyond creativity: in biology, the womb and the incubator provide controlled environments that protect and sustain development until the body is ready to emerge. CORA translates this principle into fashion, creating garments that function as temporary protective spaces where external stimulation is reduced and internal transformation can take place.

The four stages of the creative process

Graham Wallas described four stages of the creative process. Incubation is the crucial phase, where ideas develop beneath conscious awareness.

  1. 01

    Preparation

    Collecting information.

  2. 02

    Incubation

    Disconnecting externally, processing internally.

  3. 03

    Illumination

    New connections appear.

  4. 04

    Verification

    Bringing the idea into reality.

The resting brain

Incubation occurs during periods of rest, when specific brain regions known as the Default Mode Network (DMN) become active.

Research

Essential conditions for incubation

  1. 01

    Psychological distance

    Stepping away from the problem, so the mind can let go of conscious problem-solving.

  2. 02

    Reduced external stimuli

    Minimizing visual, auditory and environmental distractions to make space for internal processing.

  3. 03

    Rest

    Letting the body and mind relax through stillness, sleep or low-demand activities.

  4. 04

    Low stress

    A calm, low-pressure environment that supports relaxation and encourages creative thinking.

  5. 05

    Darkness

    Limiting exposure to light to encourage inward attention and reduce sensory stimulation.

  6. 06

    Unconscious processing

    The brain keeps processing information, forming new connections and generating insights without conscious effort.

The collection

A mobile space

The brand translates the cognitive process of incubation into a physical experience. Each piece in the collection is designed as a "mobile space" that separates the wearer from overwhelming external stimuli. Through calming weight, a reduced field of vision and black fabric, the garments create a conscious pause for the nervous system. The garment does not only represent the concept; it actively encourages the body to practice it.

01 / COCOON

Designed to create an external layer that distances the wearer from environmental stimuli, background noise, visual overload and distractions. It functions as a personal incubation space, creating a feeling of inward focus and partial disconnection from the outside world. Through material, form and coverage, the cocoon allows a transition into a mental state where thoughts, ideas and emotions can be processed without external interruption.

02 / SWADDLE

By surrounding the body, the blanket creates a temporary boundary between the inner self and the external environment. It heightens awareness of the body and of touch, shifting attention from the outside world toward the inner self, and translates incubation into a physical experience through the act of wrapping.

03 / TURGID

The garment translates incubation through body movement. Its inflated structure creates volume around the arms, reducing their range of motion and slowing the ability to act, reach and react. It forces a shift from a state of doing into a state of being. This physical limitation increases awareness of the body and redirects attention inward, creating space for thoughts and ideas to reorganize.

Materials

Material selection

A restrained material palette defines the collection. Matte black Lycra forms a second skin, while inflated textile creates volume that separates the body from its surroundings. An orange thread runs through as a continuous structural element, marking every seam with a subtle trace of warmth: a visual reminder that beneath stillness, an unseen process is taking place. Together, the materials transform incubation from a mental process into a physical experience.
