07Brand Creation

NAY

This project presents the development of a brand for bags and bowls made from plastic and discarded bags that were collected as waste and transformed through a process of reuse and material experimentation. The brand functions as an experimental lab that emphasizes sustainability, environmental awareness, and a design approach that views waste as a starting point for creativity and innovation.
